Use list() to Materialize the Generator:

print(list(x**3 for x in range(11)))
Output:
[0, 1, 8, 27, 64, 125, 216, 343, 512, 729, 1000]

Use a Loop to Iterate Over the Generator:
for value in (x**3 for x in range(11)):
    print(value)
(Same output as above)
